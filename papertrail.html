About the Book
"Hope you can hold. Evidence you can touch."

Paper Trail is a pocket companion for anyone facing harassment, retaliation, wage theft, or a hostile workplace — and isn't sure what to say next. You don't have to be certain it's "bad enough." You just have to start.

Step by step, it helps you log what happened in your own words, preserve evidence before you lose access, turn scattered moments into a clear pattern, and walk into HR or an attorney's office prepared. Plain language, room to write, and a steady voice every few pages reminding you that you're not overreacting — you're building something real.

A documentation journal, not legal advice.
