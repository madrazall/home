01

The Situation

Most electrical contractors in Connecticut are either using a generic template site from 2014, a half-finished Wix page someone's nephew set up, or nothing at all. That's actually good news — because it means a well-built site gives you a visible edge the moment someone Googles "electrician Berlin CT" at 9pm with a blown breaker.

Your clients are homeowners, property managers, and business owners. They're comparing you to whoever else shows up in search results. A professional, fast-loading, mobile-friendly site with a clear quote request form converts that comparison into a call.

The goal isn't just a website. It's a lead-generating asset that works on nights and weekends when the phone is off — and makes sure every potential customer that finds you online sees exactly why Preferred Electric is the right call.
