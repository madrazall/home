The Vision

Kelly, you've already got something real — a loyal community, a beautiful product line, and a face-to-face energy that people clearly connect with. This build is about giving that energy a home online. Not just a store, but an experience — one that feels like you walked them through it yourself.

The goal: a self-contained, easy-to-manage online store where customers can browse, vibe with the products, and check out on their own — without you having to be in the inbox for every order.

The site will lead with your crystals (your priority inventory) and feature your intention candles as the signature product line. Every product page will feel intentional — not generic. Custom orders and shipping will be built in from day one.
